# OCTIV — Oklahoma Computational Imaging & Visualization Group

Welcome to **OCTIV**, the GitHub organization for the **Oklahoma Computational Imaging & Visualization Group** in the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Oklahoma**.

We develop **high-performance optical imaging instrumentation** and **visualization tools** for biomedical and clinical applications, with strong emphasis on **Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT)** and computational methods that make imaging faster, more reliable, and more useful in practice.

---

## What we do

OCTIV’s defining strength is **integrating computation with optics**: we combine imaging physics, instrumentation, and advanced computation (e.g., optimization and machine learning) to invent new capabilities in biomedical imaging.

We also expand OCT-driven innovation into broader clinical domains (e.g., ophthalmology and cancer biology) and develop biologically relevant testbeds (e.g., tumor organoids) to support translation-focused imaging research.

---

## Core research themes

Our projects typically sit at the intersection of **imaging theory**, **system building**, and **computational algorithms**, including:

- **OCT** — imaging theory, instrumentation, and system development  
- **Computational Imaging** — hardware–algorithm co-design and device-in-the-loop calibration  
- **Biomedical Image Processing & AI** — preprocessing, segmentation, inpainting, enhancement, super-resolution, and deep learning  
- **Cancer Biology Platforms** — tumor organoids as biologically relevant testbeds for imaging innovation  
- **Computer-Generated Holography** — AR/VR-oriented holographic computation and display pipelines
